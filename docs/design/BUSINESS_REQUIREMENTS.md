# Enterprise Banking Risk Intelligence Platform (EBRIP)

## 1. Project Overview

The Enterprise Banking Risk Intelligence Platform (EBRIP) is an end-to-end banking analytics solution designed to help financial institutions monitor, assess, and manage credit risk, fraud risk, and anti-money laundering (AML) compliance.

The platform integrates multiple banking data sources into a centralized PostgreSQL data warehouse and provides analytical insights through SQL, Python, Machine Learning, Power BI dashboards, and a Streamlit-based decision support application.

---

## 2. Business Problem

Banks process millions of financial transactions and loan applications every day.

Risk management teams face several challenges:

- Identifying high-risk loan applicants before approval
- Detecting fraudulent financial transactions
- Monitoring suspicious transactions for AML compliance
- Generating regulatory reports
- Monitoring portfolio-level risk exposure
- Providing executive-level business insights

Traditional reporting systems operate in silos and make it difficult to obtain a unified view of customer risk.

This project aims to solve these challenges through an integrated analytics platform.

---

## 3. Business Objectives

The platform will enable the bank to:

- Improve loan approval decisions
